Task 3. Testing Principles
			1) Testing shows the presence of Error : Testing is find Errors in software or product but can not prove that now 
								 the software is free from error.
			2) Early testing saves time and money  : Sometime The QA is find errors and defects in software during the development
								 process  so that they save time.
			3)Exhaustive Testing is Impossible     : The tester find defects and fix them . This is impossible because of time limit,
								 cost and infinite requirements.
			4)Define clustered                     : Many more time the defects find in small products. like the 80% of defects find in 
						                 20% part of software  or product.
			5)pesticide paradox                    : The developer have to update and review the software anytime.
			6) Testing is Context Depend           : The social Media , bank management and E-commerce all testing are 
								different sides.
			7)Absence of Error Fallacy  	       : Finding and Fixing the defects.
